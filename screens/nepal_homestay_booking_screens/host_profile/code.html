About Me

Namaste! I am Kamala, though most people here call me 'Aama'. I have lived in this stone house in Dhulikhel for over forty years, watching the sun rise over the Langtang range every single morning.

My kitchen has always been the heart of my home. To me, food is more than nourishment; it is a way to share our culture and the warmth of a Himalayan hearth. I believe that every guest who walks through my door is a blessing, and I take pride in teaching my visitors the rhythm of Nepali life.

When I'm not in the kitchen, you'll find me tending to my terrace garden where I grow the organic herbs and vegetables we will eat during your stay.
