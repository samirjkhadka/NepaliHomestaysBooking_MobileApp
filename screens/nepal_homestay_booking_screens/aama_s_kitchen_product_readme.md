# Aama's Kitchen: Himalayan Hearth Homestays

Aama's Kitchen is an AI-powered homestay booking platform specifically designed for the Nepal market. Unlike generic booking sites, Aama's Kitchen (Himalayan Hearth) prioritizes cultural immersion, local impact, and mountain safety, serving as a "Digital Sherpa" for travelers.

## 🏔️ Core Product Vision
To move beyond transactional lodging and create a "Community Hearth" where every booking supports local families, preserves Himalayan heritage, and ensures traveler safety in rugged terrain.

## 🛠️ Feature Ecosystem

### 1. The Guest Journey (Discovery to Post-Stay)
*   **Home Explorer & Search**: Browse authentic homestays with a "Flavor First" approach. Includes Map View and filters for regional specialties.
*   **Homestay Details**: Deep dives into the "Morning Rituals," local crafts, and host stories that make each home unique.
*   **Booking & Payments**: A streamlined, secure flow including booking summaries, guest details, and a local payment gateway.
*   **Traveler's Dashboard**: A personalized hub spotlighting active trips, heritage progress, and local impact.
*   **Trip Itinerary**: A comprehensive view of stays, workshops, and transit details.
*   **Saved Favorites**: A curated list of "dream hearths" for future journeys.
*   **Review System**: High-fidelity community feedback focusing on hospitality and cultural authenticity.

### 2. The Host Management Suite
*   **Host Dashboard**: A central business hub for tracking earnings, upcoming guests, and property performance.
*   **Host Profile**: A storytelling-focused profile that builds trust through host heritage and expertise.
*   **Messaging Inbox**: Real-time communication between guests and hosts for seamless coordination.
*   **Manage Calendar**: Intuitive tools for setting availability, seasonal pricing, and blacking out dates.
*   **Host Earnings Report**: Transparent financial tracking and payout history.
*   **Host-to-Home Flow**: A robust multi-step onboarding process for property details, amenities, and pricing.

### 3. Unique Selling Propositions (The USPs)
*   **Himalayan Concierge**: On-trail support featuring altitude tracking, local weather, and an emergency S.O.S. link.
*   **Local Impact Dashboard**: Real-time transparency showing how booking fees fund local schools, reforestation, and trail maintenance.
*   **Cultural Immersion Tracker**: A gamified experience where guests earn badges (e.g., "Sel Roti Master") for learning local customs and phrases.
*   **Village Marketplace**: An e-commerce hub where guests can pre-order authentic pashminas, spices, and teas directly from artisans.
*   **Flavor Map**: An interactive culinary exploration tool mapping Nepal’s regional dishes and the homestays that serve them.
*   **Community Hearth (Forum)**: A social space for "Traveler Moments" and "Host Stories" to build community trust.
*   **Group Hearth**: Specialized logistics for trekking groups to manage shared expenses and trail plans.

### 4. Safety & Support
*   **Identity Verification**: Robust KYC for both guests and hosts to ensure a secure community.
*   **Check-in Instructions**: Digital arrival guides with landmark-based directions and local customs.
*   **Help Center**: A comprehensive support hub for FAQs and local emergency contact information.
*   **Settings & Safety**: Privacy controls and safety settings tailored for Himalayan travel.

## 🎨 Design Language: Himalayan Hearth
*   **Color Palette**: Deep Madder Red (#b32821), Stone Grey, and warm Cream surfaces reminiscent of traditional Nepali architecture and the national flower (Rhododendron).
*   **Typography**: *Be Vietnam Pro* for a modern yet approachable feel.
*   **Vibe**: Warm, trustworthy, grounded, and heritage-focused.
