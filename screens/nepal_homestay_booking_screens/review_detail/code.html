"An unforgettable stay with Aama Kamala"

Staying with Aama Kamala was the highlight of our trek through the Annapurna region. From the moment we arrived, we were treated like family, not just guests. The hospitality is deeply rooted in local tradition, and you can feel the warmth in every corner of her beautiful home.

Every evening, we gathered around the hearth for what I can honestly say was the best Dal Bhat I've ever tasted. The lentils were perfectly seasoned, and the local greens were so fresh they must have been picked that afternoon. We ate until we were full, and Aama still insisted on "just one more scoop!"

The views from the terrace are simply breathtaking. Waking up to the sunrise over the Machhapuchhre peak while sipping ginger tea is a memory I will cherish forever. If you want an authentic, soulful experience in Nepal, look no further.
